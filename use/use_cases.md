### docs/use_cases.md

This document outlines the primary R&D and production workflows for the **Type 1: Ferment** appliance, focusing on substrate transformation and value recovery.

---

#### 1. Mycelial Pre-Treatment (Waste Recovery)

**Objective:** Transform fibrous or bitter agricultural byproducts into high-value culinary bases.

* **Substrates:** Citrus pomace, leek trimmings, pear culls, and woody brassica stems.
* **Process:** 14–30 day incubation in **Chamber A**.
* **Mechanism:** Mycelial enzymes (specifically *Aspergillus oryzae* or specialized mushroom mycelia) degrade cellulose and neutralize bitter alkaloids.
* **Result:** A softened, umami-rich substrate suitable for purées, dehydration into powders, or further fermentation.

#### 2. Precision Koji Production

**Objective:** Rapid, aerobic inoculation of grains or legumes for enzymatic harvesting.

* **Substrates:** Polished rice, pearl barley, or steamed legumes.
* **Process:** 24–72 hour cycle in **Chamber A**.
* **Logic:** Utilization of horizontal crossflow to manage metabolic heat. The system triggers a **Level 2 Warning** if the bed temperature exceeds 35°C, preventing "burn" and ensuring protease/amylase optimization.
* **Result:** High-potency koji for immediate use in misos, shoyus, or amino sauces.

#### 3. High-Fat Thermal Enzymatic Fermentation

**Objective:** Long-term stabilization and flavor development of lipid-heavy substrates.

* **Substrates:** Macadamia nuts, walnuts, high-fat seed pastes, and koji-fortified nut butters.
* **Process:** 30–60 day cycle in **Chamber B**.
* **Mechanism:** Thermal enzymatic breakdown at 60°C.
* **Control Requirement:** Mandatory **Venting Cycles** (10–30 minutes every 6–12 hours) to flush CO₂ and introduce trace O₂, preventing lipid oxidation and anaerobic off-flavors.
* **Result:** Deeply complex, shelf-stable gourmet pastes with "cheese-like" or "cured-meat" profiles.

#### 4. Accelerated Amino Sauce (Garum)

**Objective:** Rapid proteolysis of animal or vegetable proteins.

* **Substrates:** Grass-fed beef trimmings, fish sauce bases, or roasted vegetable mashes mixed with koji.
* **Process:** 40–60 days at a sustained 55–60°C in **Chamber B**.
* **Result:** Highly concentrated umami liquids with superior clarity and refined salt profiles.

#### 5. Combined Pipeline (The Fermentation Bridge)

**Objective:** Executing a multi-stage transformation within a single unit.

1. **Stage 1:** Agricultural byproducts are pre-treated in **Chamber A** using mycelia to reduce bitterness.
2. **Stage 2:** The resulting material is salted, inoculated, and moved to **Chamber B** for a 45-day thermal ferment.
3. **Outcome:** A proprietary "Grand Reserve" paste created entirely from recovered kitchen waste.
