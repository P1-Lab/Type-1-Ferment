# HARDWARE DISCLOSURE

## Dual-Chamber Controlled Fermentation Appliance (Prior Art Specification)

---

## 1. Scope

This document describes a dual-chamber controlled-environment appliance designed for solid-state and liquid fermentation processes requiring independently regulated temperature, humidity, airflow, and atmospheric composition.

The system supports microbial fermentation including fungal and bacterial cultures under controlled environmental conditions.

---

## 2. Structural Construction

### 2.1 Chassis

* Material: AISI 316L stainless steel
* Geometry: Rectangular enclosure with radiused internal and external corners
* Purpose: Corrosion resistance and hygienic cleanability

---

### 2.2 Exterior Housing

* Structural shell: carbon steel
* Surface coating: ceramic-based powder coating
* Surface finish: matte texture
* Thermal resistance: up to 250°C (coating system dependent)
* Surface properties: non-porous, cleanable industrial finish

---

### 2.3 Thermal Insulation

* Material: rigid polyurethane foam
* Thickness: approximately 80 mm
* Function: thermal isolation between chambers and external environment

---

## 3. Chamber Architecture

The system contains two independently controlled environmental chambers.

---

## 4. Chamber A: Solid-State Fermentation Zone

### 4.1 Intended Function

Primary fermentation chamber for fungal cultures (e.g., Aspergillus oryzae) and short-duration solid-state microbial processing.

---

### 4.2 Environmental Control

* Temperature range: 20–36°C (±0.1°C control precision)
* Relative humidity: 70–95% RH (±2%)
* Airflow: forced convection with adjustable low-velocity circulation

---

### 4.3 Air Handling

* Inlet filtration: HEPA-grade particulate filtration
* Additional filtration: activated carbon VOC filtration
* Function: reduction of airborne contamination and odor exchange

---

### 4.4 Load System

* Tray configuration: multi-tier removable trays
* Tray dimensions: approximately 40 × 60 cm
* Capacity: 5–6 trays
* Maximum load: 25–50 kg depending on substrate density

---

### 4.5 Tray Material

* High-permeability composite mesh (glass-fiber based or equivalent microbial-resistant material)
* Function: airflow penetration and moisture exchange

---

## 5. Chamber B: Extended Fermentation Zone

### 5.1 Intended Function

Secondary fermentation chamber for long-duration enzymatic and bacterial fermentation processes.

---

### 5.2 Environmental Control

* Temperature range: 40–75°C (±0.2°C control precision)
* Relative humidity: 60–85% RH
* Control mode: programmable ramp and hold cycles

---

### 5.3 Atmospheric Regulation

* Gas exchange system: programmable inlet/outlet valves
* Capabilities:

  * controlled CO₂ release
  * controlled O₂ exchange
  * optional nitrogen purge capability for low-oxygen conditions
* Venting cycles: programmable intervals (typical 6–12 hour cycles)

---

### 5.4 Load System

* Shelf-based modular racks
* Shelf load capacity: approximately 10 kg per shelf
* Total chamber capacity: approximately 50 kg

---

## 6. Environmental Sterilization Systems

* Ultraviolet-C (UVC) internal irradiation system
* Steam sanitation ports capable of reaching approximately 70°C surface sterilization conditions
* Application: periodic internal decontamination of chamber surfaces

---

## 7. Control and Automation System

### 7.1 Interface

* Integrated touchscreen control panel
* Functions:

  * temperature setpoint control
  * humidity setpoint control
  * airflow modulation
  * fermentation cycle programming

---

### 7.2 Data Logging

* Real-time environmental monitoring
* Batch data export capability in CSV format
* Wireless data transmission capability

---

### 7.3 Control Resolution

* Temperature control precision: up to ±0.1°C (low-temperature chamber)
* Humidity control precision: ±2% RH

---

## 8. Electrical Specifications

* Input voltage: 220V AC
* Power consumption: approximately 2000W nominal load
* Connector type: industrial IEC 60309 standard plug

---

## 9. Physical Dimensions and Load Constraints

* External footprint: 70 cm (W) × 75 cm (D) × 180 cm (H)
* Stackability: modular stacking configuration up to two units
* Maximum stacked load: approximately 500 kg (structural dependent)

---

## 10. Functional Summary

The system provides:

* dual independent fermentation environments
* controlled solid-state fungal fermentation capability
* controlled bacterial fermentation capability
* programmable environmental cycling (temperature, humidity, gas composition)
* contamination mitigation via filtration and sterilization systems
* data logging for process repeatability

---

This document describes a mechanical and environmental control system for fermentation processes. It does not define biological strains, recipes, or proprietary formulations.
