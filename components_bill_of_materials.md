# Components Bill of Materials (High-Level)

## Sensors

- Temperature sensors (high precision RTD class or equivalent)
- Relative humidity sensors
- Optional CO₂/O₂ gas sensors

---

## Actuators

- Variable-speed axial or crossflow fans
- Resistive heating elements per chamber
- Solenoid gas exchange valves
- Ultrasonic humidification module
- UVC sterilization emitters

---

## Structural Materials

- AISI 316L stainless steel (internal surfaces)
- Carbon steel chassis (external structure)
- Polyurethane foam insulation (80 mm)

---

## Filtration

- HEPA intake filtration system
- Activated carbon VOC filtration system

---

## Control Electronics

- Embedded controller board
- Relay and power distribution modules
- Sensor aggregation system
