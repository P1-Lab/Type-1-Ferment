# Manufacturing Constraints

## Materials

- Internal chamber: AISI 316L stainless steel
- External chassis: carbon steel or stainless steel variant
- Insulation: 80 mm polyurethane foam

---

## Fabrication Methods

- Sheet metal forming
- Welded internal structure
- Modular electronics assembly

---

## Assembly Requirements

- Serviceable internal electronics bay
- Replaceable filtration modules
- Modular tray and rack system

---

## Structural Constraints

- Stackable configuration up to 2 units
- Minimum load tolerance: 500 kg stacked system (structural dependent)
