# Maintenance and Service

## Filter Replacement

- HEPA filter: 6-month cycle
- Carbon filter: 3–6 month cycle depending on VOC load

---

## Cleaning Cycles

- UVC sterilization: user-triggered or scheduled
- Steam sanitation: optional 70°C cycle for internal surfaces

---

## Inspection Schedule

- Annual inspection of:
  - seals and gaskets
  - heating elements
  - sensor calibration
  - airflow systems

---

## Service Access

- Modular panel access for:
  - sensors
  - fans
  - filtration components
