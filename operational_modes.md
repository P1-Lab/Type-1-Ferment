# Operational Modes

## Koji Mode
- Temperature: 28–35°C
- Humidity: 75–85%
- Aerobic airflow active
- High oxygen availability

---

## Mycelial Growth Mode
- Temperature: 24–28°C
- Humidity: 85–90%
- Extended duration operation (14–30 days)

---

## High-Fat Fermentation Mode
- Temperature: 60–65°C
- Controlled humidity: 75–85%
- Periodic gas vent cycles

---

## Drying Mode
- Low humidity operation
- Controlled airflow for dehydration

---

## Sterilization Mode
- UVC irradiation active
- Optional steam sanitation at ~70°C
- Chamber isolation enforced
