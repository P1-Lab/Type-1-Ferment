# Safety and Compliance

## Electrical Safety

- Industrial 220V input system
- Internal separation of low-voltage control systems

---

## Thermal Safety

- Insulated external surfaces
- Automatic thermal cutoff system

---

## Biological Safety

- HEPA filtration on intake and exhaust
- Controlled containment of fermentation aerosols

---

## UV Safety

- UVC systems fully enclosed with interlock protection

---

## Design Intent

System is designed to be compatible with general industrial appliance safety certification frameworks (e.g., CE / UL pathways depending on jurisdiction)
